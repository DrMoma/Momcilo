About.

I started Brand Layer because creative people kept getting websites that looked like everyone else's. Fifty-plus projects later, I still care about the same thing: a site should feel like the person behind it.

Running an agency taught me the craft. Running my own brands taught me the rest. Perlemor is a Norwegian jewelry brand I built from name to storefront, including the product photography, the ads, the UGC partnerships and the customer emails. LANDR is a snowboard and ski gear brand I run together with Niklas, where I handle the web, the technical side and the design. Every one of them has given me a problem an agency client would eventually hand me anyway.

That's the difference I bring. I've been on both sides of the invoice.
